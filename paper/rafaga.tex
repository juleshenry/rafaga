\section{Introduction}
Options on the VIX are written on an index that cannot be traded, that mean-reverts
strongly, and whose distribution has a heavy right tail. Bao~\citep{bao2013} models
$\ln\VIX_t$ directly as a mean-reverting process and studies four specifications:
pure diffusion (MRLR), diffusion with upward exponential jumps (MRLRJ), stochastic
vol-of-vol (MRLRSV) and both (MRLRSVJ). Using CBOE quotes from 26~September~2011,
the thesis reports that MRLR cannot produce the positive VIX skew, that jumps
\emph{or} stochastic volatility each fix this, and that combining them adds nothing.

This note (i)~provides an open Julia implementation of MRLR, MRLRJ and MRLRSV;
(ii)~checks it against the parameter tables published in the thesis, since the
original quotes are not public; (iii)~re-runs the thesis' calibration procedure,
unchanged, on the CBOE VIX chain of 29~September~2026; and (iv)~tests what the
original study did not: out-of-sample strikes, consistency with VIX futures, parameter
identification and a single set of parameters for the whole term structure.

\section{Models and pricing}
All three models share the mean-reverting log drift,
\begin{align*}
d\ln\VIX_t &= \kappa(\theta-\ln\VIX_t)\,dt + \sigma\,dW_t
  && \text{(MRLR)}\\
d\ln\VIX_t &= \kappa(\theta-\ln\VIX_t)\,dt + \sigma\,dW_t + J\,dN_t
  && \text{(MRLRJ)}\\
d\ln\VIX_t &= \kappa(\theta-\ln\VIX_t)\,dt + \sqrt{V_t}\,dW_t, && \\
dV_t &= \kappa_v(\theta_v-V_t)\,dt+\sigma_v\sqrt{V_t}\,dZ_t
  && \text{(MRLRSV)}
\end{align*}
with $N_t$ Poisson($\lambda$), $J\sim\mathrm{Exp}(\eta)$ and
$d\langle W,Z\rangle_t=\rho\,dt$. Let $\phi=e^{-\kappa\tau}$ and
$\psi(s)=\E_t[e^{is\ln\VIX_T}]$. For MRLRJ,
\begin{equation*}
\ln\psi(s) = is\mu - \tfrac12 s^2 v + \tfrac{\lambda}{\kappa}
  \ln\frac{\eta-is\phi}{\eta-is},
\end{equation*}
$\mu=\phi\ln\VIX_t+(1-\phi)\theta$, $v=\sigma^2(1-\phi^2)/2\kappa$; MRLR is the case
$\lambda=0$. For MRLRSV, $\psi=\exp(a+bV_t+is\phi\ln\VIX_t)$ where, with
$u(h)=is\,e^{-\kappa h}$, $b'=\tfrac12u^2+\rho\sigma_v u b+\tfrac12\sigma_v^2b^2-\kappa_v b$
and $a=is\theta(1-\phi)+\kappa_v\theta_v\!\int_0^\tau b$, solved by RK4 as the thesis
recommends. The VIX future is $F=\psi(-i)$ (finite for MRLRJ only if $\eta>1$) and
a call is $C=D\,(F\Pi_1-K\Pi_2)$ with the Gil-Pelaez probabilities
\begin{equation*}
\Pi_{2}=\tfrac12+\tfrac1\pi\!\int_0^\infty\!\!\frac{\operatorname{Im}\!\big(e^{-iuk}\psi(u)\big)}{u}\,du,
\quad k=\ln K,
\end{equation*}
and $\Pi_1$ the same with $\psi(u-i)/\psi(-i)$. The integrals use composite
Gauss--Legendre quadrature on one grid shared by all strikes of a maturity.

\paragraph{Verification.} A test suite checks that the Fourier pricer reproduces the
MRLR closed form ($10^{-8}$), that MRLRJ and MRLRSV collapse to MRLR in their nested
limits, that prices are decreasing, convex and within arbitrage bounds, that Euler
Monte Carlo prices (200k paths) agree within four standard errors for MRLRJ and
MRLRSV, and that the RK4 step is converged. A 25-digit \texttt{BigFloat} quadrature
agrees with the double-precision pricer to $10^{-9}$, so extended precision is not needed.

\section{Consistency with the published tables}
The 2011 quotes are not available, so the fitting errors of the thesis cannot be
recomputed. The published parameters can still be tested: Tables~7.5 (MRLRJ) and
7.6 (MRLRSV) were fitted separately to the \emph{same} quotes, with reported mean
absolute errors (MAE) of 0.12--0.21. Priced with our code ($\VIX_t=42.3$, strikes
20--90), the two structurally different models agree to within 2--3.5 cents at every
maturity (Table~\ref{tab:2011}), well inside the band implied by the reported
errors, while MRLR (Table~7.4) differs by 8--17 cents. Implied volatilities against
the model future are flat for MRLR and upward-sloping for MRLRJ and MRLRSV, at the
levels shown in the thesis' Figure~7.3. We conclude that the implementation follows
the thesis' conventions.
